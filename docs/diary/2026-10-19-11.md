# Diary — 2026-10-19 (11)

## Roadmap 0012: text layout promoted from ask

Decision and numbers: ADR-0025.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Grapheme iteration dominates wrapping | `wrap/…` benchmarks on the moved code, unchanged | **CONFIRMED**: 56 ns a byte, 213 allocations for 4 KB |
| A byte walk wraps identically | the original kept as an oracle: 27 texts × 9 widths, 3,000 generated strings | **CONFIRMED**, after the oracle itself proved it can fail |
| …including CRLF | `"\r\n"` is one Character, not a line feed | **CONFIRMED**: a carriage return never takes the fast path; removing that guard fails the oracle |
| Prose is nearly all fast-path | benchmark | **CONFIRMED**: 12× faster, 4.6 ns a byte. Mixed CJK/English only 2.7× |
| The cache's contract is testable on its own | `LayoutCache` tests against a stub | **CONFIRMED**: red, then green |

The oracle passed on its first run, which proves nothing; the two deliberate breaks (NUL as one column, CR on
the fast path) are what showed it could fail.

`wrap/…` reports one call per sample, not per 1,000 like the width suites, so it sets its own configuration.
