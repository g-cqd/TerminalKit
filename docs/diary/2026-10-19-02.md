# Diary — 2026-10-19 (02)

## Roadmap 0006: a time benchmark suite

`Benchmarks/` is a separate package on package-benchmark 1.38.0, depending on TerminalKit by path. It
holds three executables: input decoding, rendering, and Unicode width. Decision and thresholds: ADR-0017.

## What the reference run says (REFERENCE.md, load average 6)

- **One-cell flush, 0008:** 2.0 µs at 80×24, 3.8 µs at 120×40, 15.5 µs at 300×80. About 0.6 ns per cell
  of screen for a change of one cell.
- **Unchanged rewrite, 0007:** 68 µs at 120×40, 334 µs at 300×80. About 14 ns per cell.
- **Decode, per event:** text 29 ns, arrow key 39 ns, Kitty key 116 ns, SGR mouse 131 ns, a 1 KiB paste
  246 ns with its one `String`.
- **New: `UnicodeWidth` costs 139 ns per CJK grapheme,** against 11 ns for ASCII and 71 ns for emoji.
  CJK is the slowest case, slower than emoji sequences. Recorded for 0013; not investigated here.
