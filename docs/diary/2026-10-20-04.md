# Diary — 2026-10-20 (4)

## Roadmap 0017: the selection model and the clipboard plan

Decision: ADR-0030. Code: `ContentSelection.swift` and `RowMap.swift` in TerminalKitText, `ClipboardPlan.swift` in TerminalKitInput;
tests `ContentSelectionTests` and `ClipboardPlanTests`; benchmarks `selection/*` in the text suite. Not yet painted into a screen.

| # | Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A selection survives scrolling and re-wrapping | the same selection over the same content at three scroll positions and three widths | **CONFIRMED**: the same text, and the highlight follows what is visible (and is empty when none is) |
| 2 | A cell maps to the right grapheme with wide characters and tabs | CJK at two cells each, a tab to the next stop, a wrapped line | **CONFIRMED**, through the kit's `TextDisplayMetrics` |
| 3 | The clipboard follows what was measured | the recorded `TERM` and `TERM_PROGRAM` of each terminal, and the plan for each | **CONFIRMED**: kitty and Ghostty one write, Terminal.app the pasteboard only, WezTerm the first write twice, unknown both, remote never the pasteboard |
| 4 | The tests can fail | ten mutations across selection and clipboard | **ALL CAUGHT**, after redoing two bad ones |
| 5 | Mapping a cell is cheap | `selection/position` | **CONFIRMED**: 692 ns ASCII, 1,044 ns CJK, zero allocations |
| 6 | Highlighting a screen is cheap | `selection/highlights`, 40 rows | **CONFIRMED in time** (36 and 58 µs) **but it allocates 86 times**, which I did not investigate |

### What went wrong

- **Two of my mutations were wrong.** One removed a use of a variable and failed to compile, which proved nothing; one was built
  backwards, making the guard fire for everything, and failed fifteen tests for the wrong reason. Both were redone.
- **One real mutation was caught by a crash, not an assertion**: putting the selection out of reading order makes a closed range
  invalid and traps the test process. It is detection, but a bad failure mode, and the code does not guard the range itself.
- **Nothing here has met a real layout.** The tests build a stand-in layout, and the provenance a real one would have to record, cheaply
  and every frame, is the biggest untested assumption.

### Not verified

Painting, dragging, autoscroll, a selection across a fold or an image, a drag across two blocks in a real terminal with the clipboard read
back, tmux, ssh and the primary selection.
